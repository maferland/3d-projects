# Elumara mold box

The panels are mesh-validated; physical leak and pour tests remain pending.
The silicone quantities below are estimates, not validated offset-volume measurements.

This is a close-fitting, inexpensive mold box for the approximately
149 x 149 x 74.4 mm Elumara lamp base.

## Recommended dimensions

- Internal cavity: 175 x 175 x 90 mm
- Minimum side clearance: about 13 mm nominal
- Silicone volume: about 2.0 L after subtracting the 770 mL master
- Buy/mix about 2.2 L to cover seams and measurement error

## Cheapest construction

Print two `long` and two `short` panels from `elumara-mold-box.scad`. Use PETG,
four perimeters, 0.24-0.28 mm layers, and no supports. The ribs face outward.
The 1.2 mm skin should resolve to an exact number of layers in the slicer;
adjust `skin` if needed.

Ready-to-slice files are in `exports/`. Print two copies of each STL.

Use a flat melamine shelf offcut, acrylic, or HDPE cutting board as the base.
Do not print a large base plate unless necessary; it costs more and is more
likely to warp.

1. Seal the printed master before molding.
2. Mark a centered 175 x 175 mm square on the base board.
3. Fix the master in the center, flat face down.
4. Place the two short panels between the long panels, ribs outward.
5. Tape the outside corners, then hot-glue every outside seam to the base.
6. Add binder clips at the corners and along the bottom edges.
7. Leak-test with water before mixing silicone; dry completely afterward.

Apply a compatible release agent if the silicone supplier requires it. Confirm
material compatibility with a small cure test, especially with resin prints.
